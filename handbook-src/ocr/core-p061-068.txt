The principle Of common but differentiated responsibilities
(CBDR) is a cornerstone of global climate governance. Given
the wholeness of the global ecosystem and the different causes
0f global ecological degradation, all countries should take
on different obligations while pursuing the common goal of
protecting the glObal environment. By "common responsibilities,'
all countries ， regardless of their size ， wealth ， and capabilities, shall
be duty-bound tO protect the environment. By "differentiated
responslbilities," the specific duties they each shoulder are framed
in terms of their capabilities. HistoricaIIy, developed countries and
developing countries have contributed different amounts tO the
damage tO the earth's envlronment ， and face different pressure when
responding tO climate change. They also bear different historical
responsibilities for causing climate change. Their development
needs and capacities vary as well. On the whole ， developed countries
should take on greater responsibilities than developing countries in
addressing climate change. China advocates the CBDR principle,
stands for fair and equitable shares of the benefits, and calls for a
new system for global climate governance whereby every country
does its best based on its own conditions and capabilities.
