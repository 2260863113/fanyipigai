The term means seeking points Of agreement while maintaining difference Of opinion.
SchoIars of the Warring States Period such as Hui Shi and Zhuang Zi believed that differences
and antitheses between all things are relative ， and that commonalities exrst within differences.
The Confucian thought represented by Confucius stressed that in relations between
people and between states, "harmony without uniformity" should be achieved through
accommodation ， provided that differences between the parties are recognized. The greatest
possible consensus between all sides is ultimately reached by acknowledging the existence
of differences or setting them aside. To achieve such consensus ， people should recognize the
diversity Of cultures and values and not seek absolute unanimity or forced agreement; and
they should look at problems as much as possible from the perspective of others.
